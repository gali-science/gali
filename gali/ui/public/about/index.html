Where your data goes.

When you try it free, the PDF file stays in your browser. Gali sends its text and your questions to DeepSeek V4.1 Flash through Together AI. If you start a voice call, it runs through OpenAI. Your chat is not saved.

With full access, your projects live in a private sandbox that only you can open. Voice, typed chats, and what is on screen go through OpenAI. Answers about the PDF, reviews, and code runs use Kimi K3 and DeepSeek V4.1 Flash through Together AI. Gali saves your last conversation so it can remember it.

Gali is a research preview, so please don’t upload anything confidential. Read the Together AI Privacy Policy.

Questions

Do I need to sign up?

Not to try it: drop a PDF and ask Gali about it. Everything else needs full access for now: reviews, code runs, the To study list, saved notes, and your own private sandbox. To get it, join the waitlist. We email you when you're in.

What can I upload?

A PDF up to 100 MB. With full access, you can add code: a zip up to 100 MB or a GitHub link.

Does it catch everything?

No. Gali can be wrong. Our guide shows where AI review helps, where it fails, and how to check it. When you try it free, open the guide at the top of the page before you add a PDF. With full access, it is in the sidebar on a computer.

What can I take with me?

Your findings as text, your notes as Markdown, and the PDF with your highlights.

What does it cost?

Trying it is free, up to a limit. With full access, you get a funded trial, and your work stays when it ends.

Is Gali open source?

Yes. The code is on GitHub. Answers about the PDF, reviews, and code runs use open models through Together AI.
